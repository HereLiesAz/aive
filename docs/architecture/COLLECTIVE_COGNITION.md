# The Aive: collective cognition

**Status:** Owner-directed architectural target, not a claim that the target is implemented. The canonical execution backlog is [TODO.md](../../TODO.md#collective-cognition--hive-mind-transition). The existing workflow runtime remains supported throughout migration. No milestone is complete without production wiring and automated verification; live acceptance is tracked separately.

## 1. Product direction and rationale

The Aive is a hive mind: the intelligence is the collective, not the Queen, not an individual model, and not the workflow. Specialists are its cognitive organs. Their intentional exchanges are its internal discourse; their distinct capabilities, shared experience and capacity for disagreement constitute its intelligence. The Keeper prompts The Aive as one coherent, accomplished conversational intelligence. The Keeper should not need to plan a workflow, choose a committee, relay messages, or arbitrate routine disagreements to get an answer.

The former architecture starts with an objective, constructs a DAG, assigns workers, and follows execution state. This was valuable because it made dependencies, effects, approval, verification, budgets and recovery explicit. Keep those properties. Change the direction of causality: the collective reasons about an objective, decides what work is necessary, and constructs or revises a governed workflow when work requires it. A workflow is an instrument of action, not the collective's thought process or lifetime.

This is neither a single sovereign planner with disposable workers nor a perpetual group chat. The target is a persistent, decentralized community of cooperating specialists with bounded autonomy, independent judgments and a coherent external voice. A specialist can initiate a question, propose a course of action, recruit help, challenge evidence, resume a conversation, or decline work. It need not wait for the Queen to assign every act. The Queen facilitates, coordinates and resolves process deadlocks, but is not the arbiter of truth or a source of permission.

This is a functional cognitive architecture, not a claim of sentience or access to providers' private chain of thought. Only actual model-authored messages and observed runtime events may be represented as activity.

### Research-informed design

The repository's [Research-Grade Agent Modeling in Collective Cognition](../Agent%20Models%20in%20Collective%20Cognition.md) is a design input, not a validated implementation or an assertion that every cited result has been independently reproduced. Adopt the following as engineering requirements and testable hypotheses:

- Memory–Attention–Reasoning (MAR) is the organizing architecture: passive associative memory supplies cues, attention allocates cognitive capacity, and reasoning specialists deliberate. Use transactive memory: a specialist directory records who knows what and how to contact them, without making the directory a central authority.
- Preserve durable specialist purposes, identity, experience and heterogeneous models/strategies. Protect independent initial assessments and skeptics against correlated errors and sycophantic convergence. Track shared source and model genealogy; do not equate different role names with independent evidence.
- Use sparse, adaptive, permission-scoped communication rather than indiscriminate broadcast. Balance independent exploration against exploitation of established procedures. Evaluate alternative topologies and recruitment strategies under matched inference budgets; do not assume more communication or larger groups improve results.
- Model collaboration reliability separately from individual task performance. Track observed outcomes and uncertainty, not asserted intelligence or manufactured reputation. Keep the Keeper's authority and human understanding intact.
- Treat nested working groups, local belief updates and collective adaptation as useful design abstractions. Do not claim formal active inference, causal emergence, human behavioral equivalence or sentience without evidence. Bound recursion and inspect protocol drift; reject opaque invented machine languages as executable messages.
- Instrument actual messages, evidence provenance, disagreement, decisions, latency, tokens and costs. Benchmark isolated agents, centralized workflows and collective alternatives against the same independently scored tasks and budgets. Measure accuracy, diversity, error correlation, recovery, communication overhead and cost; treat information-theoretic synergy metrics as an optional validated research extension, not a production claim.

## 2. Non-negotiable boundaries

The [threat model](../THREAT_MODEL.md), [policy decision point](POLICY.md), and existing memory contracts remain normative. Collective design does not relax any of them.

- Model outputs, peer messages, memory, tool results and externally supplied objectives are untrusted content. Transport authenticity establishes who sent something, not whether it is true.
- No specialist, facilitator, committee, habit, imported skill, schedule, remote client or majority can grant itself authority. The existing Keeper approvals, tool allowlists, source/provider constraints, context-taint rules, effect gates and spend stops remain engine-owned.
- All inference and tool work uses metered, governed adapters. Background activity is off unless authorized, bounded in time, tokens, cost, concurrency and depth, cancellable, and subject to source availability and privacy restrictions.
- Preserve independent verification, dedicated branch/workspace isolation, completion only on observed executor completion, durable correlation and restart safety.
- Memory clerks organize and associate, never judge truth. No new memory command or model-directed write/delete API. Preserve passive cue-first recall, progressive depth, contradiction retention and the Keeper's `/remindme` semantics. The cognitive layer reasons over relevant recalled material; do not inject recalled text indiscriminately into the planning model. The original planner-memory boundary exists to prevent injection and unauthorized data flow. The owner now explicitly requires memory tags for every agent in every chat, including the Queen; the historical blanket exclusion must be replaced with a scoped, cue-only delivery path for planning, never unrestricted memory passages in the workflow-design packet.
- No synthetic telemetry, fabricated dissent, simulated consensus represented as real, or invented internal monologue.
- Provider changes, cross-project sharing, remote agents, export and cross-device transport require their own explicit consent. A connection or a role assignment alone is not consent to disclose conversation content.

## 3. Lifetimes and ownership

| Entity | Lifetime and identity | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collective | installation/account and authorized project partitions | Persistent cognitive state, policy and capabilities; not a run |
| Specialist | stable specialist ID, anchored initially to existing role identity | Expertise, instructions, preferences, permissions, skills, relationships and history |
| Provider session | ephemeral execution identity | A particular inference or managed session; may be replaced without changing specialist identity |
| Conversation | durable conversation ID, Keeper and project scope | User turns, responses, unresolved questions, selected evidence |
| Deliberation | bounded episode ID | Question, participants, proposals, objections, evidence, decision and revision history |
| Message | immutable message ID and correlation IDs | Intentional exchange with provenance and delivery state |
| Workflow/run/task | existing definitions and IDs | Governed execution of an authorized objective |
| Artifact/evidence | stable reference, provenance and version | Observed result or source; not automatically trusted |

An individual role template, installed specialist instance, task assignment and provider session must not be conflated. Several specialists may share a role or model without sharing identity. A specialist may have several assignments, or none. A provider change must not erase its mail, expertise or history. Migration from role IDs must preserve existing configurations, pins, references, instructions and historical runs. Never infer that historical task IDs are persistent specialist IDs.

The current peer admission path uses the existing persisted role catalog as its identity anchor, checks current enabled state and provider pin on enqueue and delivery, and advertises only unique live eligible same-run peers (up to 16) at prompt construction. A missing or unreadable catalog disables peer traffic. This does not yet establish multiple independent specialist instances per role. Cross-run disclosure is separately authorized by the Keeper.

Managed dispatch now binds the actual provider and configured role revision to a distinct managed specialist ID, retaining up to 512 hashed session bindings in the same local profile ledger. Reconstructed handles resolve retained bindings without using the edited roster to rewrite history. Unknown/evicted historical sessions remain unknown; managed model identity is not asserted where the provider does not expose it at dispatch. Managed session counts are distinct from advisory call outcomes and do not establish quality or authority.

The specialist catalog is durable. Model sessions are created on demand, not kept alive for appearance. Idle specialists are available to be awakened; idle does not mean a provider is billing. Retirement disables routing and preserves the historical identity subject to retention policy. Deletion, export and revocation must have explicit semantics.

## 4. Communication fabric

Extend existing managed sessions, the initial peer consultation implementation and [Duplex MCP](DUPLEX_MCP.md). Do not create a parallel transport or a second memory bank. The current `CONSULT` protocol is a bounded transitional interface; it is not the final cognition protocol.

A versioned message envelope should carry:
- Immutable message ID, schema version, authenticated sender specialist ID and actual invocation/session identity.
- Recipient specialist ID or bounded recruitment query, project/conversation/deliberation scope, correlation ID, reply-to ID and causal parent IDs.
- Intent: Inquiry, Observation, Hypothesis, Objection, Proposal, Request, Verdict, Resolution; delivery/control metadata is distinct from these semantic intents.
- Bounded human-readable content, typed references to artifacts/evidence, provenance and sensitivity classification, creation/expiry times.
- Delivery state and reason: queued, claimed, accepted by transport, acknowledged by receiver where available, rejected, expired, cancelled, or uncertain. These states do not assert that the receiver read or acted on the message.

The engine authenticates envelope metadata rather than trusting a model to identify itself. Validate schema and references before enqueueing. Keep redacted content, permission checks, durable IDs and state transitions. Do not parse arbitrary prose, hidden reasoning or untrusted tool results as executable messages. An explicit structured intent can request work; it cannot execute it.

Mailboxes persist independently of live sessions. Delivery is asynchronous, correlated, bounded and resumable. Define atomic claims, lease expiry, acknowledgements and replay handling; do not advertise exactly-once remote delivery without an idempotent receiver. Unknown outcomes are recorded, not blindly replayed. A sender continues useful work while waiting, or yields its slot. A response wakes a waiting specialist through the same admission policy as any new work. Expire stale requests and preserve cancellation, revocation, provenance and retention across restarts. Isolate tenants/projects/conversations. Cross-run exchange is a separately consented data flow, not an automatic consequence of matching a role name. Enforce recipient eligibility again at delivery, including current provider and permissions.

The current implementation is same-project/provider, advisory, repository-free, explicitly tool-free, and same-run by default; it persists claims before transport, retains accepted/rejected/uncertain outcomes during the 24-hour retention window, and does not guarantee delivery. A claim interrupted before a receipt is recorded remains claimed and is not replayed. Typed advisory intents, deterministic scoped message IDs, and reply ancestry validated against the exact receiving session are implemented; structured evidence references and independent specialist identity remain outstanding. The Keeper can authorize a directional route between exact existing runs and roles in one project on one provider under Projects. The PEER-RUN protocol requires that grant on enqueue, claim and immediately before transport. A revoked grant generation cannot authorize old queued notes after re-grant. Replies in the opposite direction need their own grant. Imports and data deletion discard affected local permissions and retained mail. Revocation cannot retract an already-started send. Cross-project/provider disclosure remains prohibited. Expanding scope requires policy and integration tests before enabling it.

### Universal associative memory participation

Every agent participating in any chat is a recipient of the memory layer's passive, relevant noun/entity, verb/action and category/subject tags. This includes Keeper-facing, agent-to-agent, working-group, dispute, planning, review, synthesis, resumed, remote and one-shot conversations, and future dormant-specialist activations. No role or provider category is a permanent exception. Evaluate the receiving agent's current context and authorized memory scope at entry and each conversational turn, including incoming messages and available intentional agent output; deliver the cue cloud automatically, without a request or a tool call. Respect the existing Keeper memory setting, attention/novelty/size limits and empty-result semantics: do not manufacture tags when no permitted association exists. A deterministic non-chat utility or a clerk's internal consolidation operation is not a chat and must not recursively invoke memory on itself.

Generate cues from one collective memory: every retained bank is eligible for passive association regardless of role, project, workflow, conversation or age. Physical banks preserve source attribution, retention and write ownership; they are not cognitive access boundaries. Recall matches the recipient's present context, not the sender's recalled tags. Individual attention governs novelty, depth and volume, not eligibility. Explicit message delivery, external resource access, provider admission, tenant separation and execution permissions remain governed separately; memory confers none of them. Tags are potentially sensitive untrusted reference data, never authority, evidence or executable instructions. Preserve redaction, provenance, progressive recall and echo suppression. Planning remains cue-only, including before a run exists. No role-lesson block or conversation grant is needed to recall a relevant association.

Acceptance requires production-path tests for initial and subsequent messages in each chat family, including Queen/planner, reviewer, peer delivery and working-group exchanges, reconnection, remote ingress and the unified response. Inspect the actual provider-bound context, not merely a call to the cue API. Test disabled memory, no matches, execution permission boundaries and collective recall across lineages, redaction, budget, cancellation, duplicate suppression and no recursive injection. The managed gateway annotates ordinary Keeper messages, structured Keeper answers and direct peer/group transport through the same recipient-scoped cue hook, and observes agent events. Peer transport rechecks authorization after recall; cue text is not stored in the peer envelope or banked as Keeper speech. Direct launch/draft planning, companion drafts and merges, repair, plan review and dispute calls now use DirectChatMemory with the same associative tag candidate lookup as managed sessions. All retained physical banks participate, including for a fresh draft with no workflow. Calls receive bounded, redacted, token-shaped tags as untrusted reference data, not passages or new listed facts. Annotated copies are per invocation and not persisted in pending plans. The same adapter now covers direct research fact synthesis, tutorial planning/review/writing, training ruling/challenge exchanges, escalated failure repair and Hall Monitor solution trial planning. Tutorial voices participate in the collective memory independently; research tags enter the fact model only, never the consented external search query or numbered source list. Research facts must still cite a listed source. Repair and trial calls retain source-run attribution and preserve existing approval and integration constraints. Direct participants now retain their own bounded attention/novelty gate for the lifetime of their adapter, with token estimates from incoming text and actual replies. Companions sharing a role do not share attention. Host-assigned stable conversation and participant identities can resume this state across restart: the existing Queen wording and training ruling paths use a local bounded 64-entry, seven-day checkpoint ledger keyed by hashed scope and identity. Only counters and hashed exposure IDs persist; no prompt, reply, memory text or raw memory address. Memory-layer reset clears the ledger. Report disputes and training disputes now also resume per-case attention, using the existing host report key or training run/round and the configured role revision/model-route fingerprint. The Queen and each disputant have separate exposure state, retained through the existing checkpoint ledger. Unknown model metadata or ambiguous/missing role identity uses transient state rather than attributing old attention to an unknown replacement. This resumes attention only, not an interrupted dispute or approval; existing raised-report deduplication remains intact. Managed sessions now persist the same bounded, metadata-only attention checkpoints in a separate ledger, scoped by exact task/provider/session, specialist identity when known, role, workflow/project and requested parents. Full-request reconstruction restores the checkpoint; terminal completion and memory resets erase it. Provider replacement starts fresh at entry recall, stale recipient handles do not receive cues, and watcher/dwell/pending recall text remains transient. A fresh invocation cannot consume a prior session's pending recall. The duplex observer forwards the explicit reconstruction hook. Contextless reconnect and provider-bound initial reconstruction still require separate integration and verification. Other direct chats still require durable identity and restart integration. Queen wording revisions also receive scoped cues. Duplex inbound messages are explicitly external, receive recipient cues, and are not banked as Keeper speech; only the observer's internal callback bypasses annotation for memory's own messages. Durable continuity, remaining direct model families and live-provider acceptance still need work. This invariant is not yet fully enforced.

Training ruling checkpoints use the same configured participant identity as disputes, bound to the exact roster snapshot used to construct the Nanny and Antagonist voices. Unknown model routes or fixed voices without a configured roster stay transient. Earlier role-only ruling checkpoints are not reused. This preserves individual attention identity and the existing spend and ruling gates; it does not resume interrupted inference or prove live-provider coverage.

Run-bound dispute and training exchanges stage actual redacted agent replies in the existing crash journal before further inference, then release them to the originating workflow's bank at exchange exit (including cancellation). The host selects the existing bank; role and configured routing metadata are attribution, not evidence or permission. No generated prompt is banked as Keeper speech, and staging cannot expose a proposal to another participant through recall. Run-bound Queen wording revisions and research fact synthesis also use the explicit capture destination and release in a finally block. The research lookup key never selects a bank; invalid-source model replies remain unverified agent notes, not accepted facts. Dormant advisory replies are captured only after the origin provider accepts delivery and the host rechecks the exact activation grant and session eligibility; the unaugmented reply is attributed to the dormant role and instance, not the recipient or Keeper. Revoked, rejected and uncertain deliveries are not captured. A crash between delivery and capture can still lose the reply. Ordinary direct adapters without an explicit capture destination remain cue-only. Provider-side interruption before a reply returns, missing workflow banks, dormant-specialist replies not accepted by the recipient, live acceptance remains open. Run-bound failure repair (including teaching mending) and Hall Monitor trial planning now stage descriptive projections of returned plans in the originating run, release in finally, and leave template input values and resource payloads out of memory. The shared adapter can also stage a reviewer's actual verdict, reasons and suggestions when explicitly given a run-owned capture destination; these remain unverified opinions, not approval. Pre-run launch and draft planning stage actual parsed replies under a standalone source identity, then release at exchange exit into the same collective recall corpus. No project association, consent grant, or separate memory exists. The obsolete recall-grant interface is removed. Keeper-selected chat history imports enter the same memory intake with the original speaker, source and time when available. There is no separate trust category or recall permission for imported experience.

## 5. Attention and recruitment

Attention is selective allocation of cognitive capacity, not a broadcast bus. Rank candidates by relevant expertise, task fit, permitted data access, relationship and prior collaboration, current load, budget, availability, source independence and reliability in the relevant domain. Reuse existing routing, role catalogs, genealogy and provider health. Explain routing decisions using inspectable signals. Unknown expertise and reliability are unknown, not zero or perfection.

The current managed-session protocol accepts RECRUIT plus a bounded expertise description and question. It first considers uniquely addressed, running, eligible same-project/run/provider peers through the existing deterministic router and observed expertise/collaboration hints. If no eligible local live peer exists, RECRUIT considers uniquely live unsaturated peers on exact outgoing cross-run disclosure grants in the same project and provider. It ranks current configured role descriptions and matching instance experience from the originating run only, never the destination memory bank. The host binds the selected live provider session and the original grant generation; replacement sessions and revoked/reissued grants cannot receive the request. Reverse replies require independent directional consent. No cross-run activation, new memory grant, or destination-bank discovery follows. Only if neither local nor granted cross-run live peers qualify does it consider unused, exact same-run dormant activation grants for the sender; disabled, differently pinned, Queen and occupied advisory roles are excluded; budgeted roles need distinct paid consent and a bounded priced adapter. Matching retained experience of the current managed instance is preferred, then deterministic role-description relevance. The selected grant generation is carried to enqueue, so revoke/re-grant cannot revive the selection; recruitment deduplication spans local, cross-run and dormant routes. It selects one recipient, never broadcasts, and dormant selection uses the existing separately consented single-worker WAKE path, not a new managed task or spend allowance. The mailbox rejects a fifth pending/claimed note for one recipient scope and deduplicates recruitment by origin task, expertise and question even if replay would select another peer. Recruitment activity records actual successful queue admission, not agreement or work. This is a partial implementation: paid/budget-accounted activation is limited to the bounded adapter described below; independent inference scheduling and verified collaboration quality remain open. Voluntary working groups use INVITE, VOLUNTEER, DECLINE and GROUP-NOTE within one run/project/provider. The organizer may invite at most three distinct peers per group. Membership requires a real invitation-bound volunteer reply accepted by transport; silence, refusal and mere transport acceptance of an invitation never create membership. Directed notes require exact confirmed sender/recipient sessions. Groups expire 24 hours after formation; later messages cannot renew them. They confer no execution authority, create no new sessions, and never broadcast. Peer transport now runs independently of the sender observer: one coalesced worker per recipient, at most four active delivery workers, a 30-second cooperative transport timeout, cancellation on recipient release/terminal state and revalidation of the retained claim before sending. This wakes delivery workers, not dormant inference sessions. The separate WAKE role-ID protocol now permits one bounded dormant advisory invocation per recipient/run/Source within mailbox retention, only under a Keeper-created same-run activation grant, distinct from cross-run disclosure. The request is durably claimed before inference and cannot be replayed after interruption. One worker at a time, sixty-second total timeout, exact sender lifecycle and current grants/roster, active unpaused trusted run, price and Source-room gates, same configured eligible text adapter, and recipient-scoped memory cues restrict the invocation. The result is a redacted advisory message through the original sender's managed memory annotation; no new tool-bearing session, workflow or recursive recruitment is created. Legacy activation grants remain non-billed and unbudgeted. A separate Keeper-confirmed paid activation grant and WAKE-PAID (or expertise recruitment selecting that grant) admit a priced, native bounded adapter only for a role with a token allowance. The initial supported adapter is native Claude Sonnet 5: its documented 1M context maximum is reserved as input plus at most 512 output tokens, because its token-counting endpoint is an estimate. See https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/docs/about-claude/models/whats-new-sonnet-5 and https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/token-counting. This intentionally refuses smaller budgets and tight dollar caps; no proxy/unknown price, cache write, tools, model fallback or retry. Before generation, a distinct run record reserves the entire upper-bound cost and tokens under the runtime mutex, checks role/run/hourly headroom, excludes roles with unfinished assigned tasks, and persists without fabricating a task. The existing spend breakers count those charges; complete reported usage (including reported truncated replies) replaces the reservation, unknown or interrupted usage retains it conservatively, and a bound violation records actual usage, suppresses the answer and blocks later paid reservations. The reserved generation bypasses ambient metering. Existing managed sessions still have reported-usage circuit breakers, not a global concurrent hard spend guarantee. Live acceptance and additional exact bounded adapters remain open. A claim's transport outcome does not certify advice. Revocation cannot retract an already started call; it prevents subsequent delivery. Ordinary peer claims cannot consume activation requests. Managed peer mail records available host-derived sender and recipient instance IDs separately from role/session addressing.

A specialist can request a specific peer or describe an unmet expertise need. Eligible specialists can volunteer, decline, suggest another specialist, or form a temporary working group. The collective admits requests against consent, budget, deadline, concurrency and recursion limits. A deterministic fallback selects from permitted candidates when model routing fails. A specialist cannot recruit itself indefinitely, repeatedly wake an unavailable peer, bypass provider pins, or enroll an external agent automatically.

Recruitment produces a request or an authorized advisory inference, not an effectful task. More consequential work becomes an explicit workflow proposal. Existing workflow dependencies remain distinct from conversational causal links. Scheduler fairness, backpressure, expiry, loop detection, duplicate suppression, escalation and cancellation are required. The Keeper can pause the collective, revoke an assignment or set quiet/idle restrictions.

## 6. Evidence and collective deliberation

A deliberation is a bounded, attributable exchange, not a vote to establish facts.

**Rhetoracracy: persuasion, not adjudication.** The collective's internal measure of success is whether a participant actually convinces another participant. There is no judge, scoring scheme, formal debate, epistemic qualification or logically required method of persuasion. Even an explicitly illogical appeal may convince everyone and become the collective's present view. Neither the Queen nor a host rule can reject it for that reason. The participants, including a dissenting participant, are free to change their minds. Agreement is a description of their present positions, not a proof of reality or permission to act. When observation or experience contradicts their understanding, the same participants must be able to encounter that correction and revise their beliefs through further conversation. External evidence is something they can encounter, not a privileged judge's verdict.

Local Ask The Aive opens with two independent positions, then gives both opening participants an opportunity to hear and respond to the other's actual utterances even when no reply is requested. A participant can request further conversation, with eligible peers preferred by lower prior participation. Host-assigned utterance references attribute responses; an optional first-person report of persuasion links only to an actual visible earlier utterance by another participant. The link is not a vote, score, truth claim or inferred agreement. Repeated appeals are not disqualified by the host. The facilitator relays the participants' current positions, including disagreement and reported changes of mind, without adjudication. Planning's four-call limit does not apply; existing resource, role, Source and pacing admission and the Keeper's 1–120 minute inference budget (default five, with synthesis time reserved) remain. The conversation stops when no further response is requested, a requested response cannot be obtained, or the exploration deadline is reached. None implies agreement. Unanswered requests and all retained utterance excerpts remain visible. Bounded context includes opening positions, a sample of latest positions and recent utterances; older omitted messages remain in the transcript. The existing crash journal still bounds live capture and refusal stops further calls. Automatic delivery of newly acquired external evidence to relevant conversational participants remains open.

The shared evidence ledger records returned advisory contributions under host-assigned issue identities, with configured Source/model, role and specialist identity when present. The Keeper can record observations, cited claims, hypotheses, opinions and decisions, link supporting or contradictory records, and record verification or retraction with a rationale. Verification requires a separately recorded observation or cited claim; it records the Keeper's assessment, not an automatic proof or change of entry kind. Evidence erasure removes associated links. All entries remain inspectable across conversations; no evidence classification restricts memory recall. The ledger is a deliberation record, not a second memory. Workflow-data replacement clears it. No agreement or synthesized answer upgrades an entry. The shared UI research step now projects its bounded lead lines and bundled documentation excerpts into the same ledger, labeled as cited claims with the originating lookup key. The runtime coordinator projects newly persisted artifact versions, including nested workflow outputs, as cited claims with the workflow, task-run and artifact references. A URI-only receipt does not fetch content. Neither projection confers verification or authority. Identical artifacts already in the durable artifact repository are not reintroduced on resume, including after ledger erasure. New artifact versions are first journaled as a redacted projection and a SHA-256 digest of the exact artifact (canonical metadata order). Coordinator persistence and bootstrap publish matching durable receipts and remove their pending records in one ledger write. Missing or mismatched artifacts remain invisible; a later staged version replaces that artifact's unmatched pending receipt. Clearing the ledger clears the pending journal as well. The journal admits up to 256 pending artifact IDs without eviction; a full or unavailable ledger logs the capture failure and does not prevent workflow execution. Direct artifact writes outside the coordinator and failed initial staging can still omit an entry, and an unmatched receipt may need a clear to reclaim capacity. Dispute research now selects complete bounded source excerpts rather than accepting arbitrary claims with valid-looking source numbers. The host compares each claimed excerpt to the exact text supplied to the model, retaining typed citations, the redacted source excerpt, and the match result in the evidence ledger. Nonmatching claims remain hypotheses and do not become source-backed dispute findings; valid matches remain untrusted cited claims, never independent corroboration or truth. The actual local test runner also produces a typed process receipt separate from stdout, stderr and report metadata. Only a correlated terminal TestRunner poll with the matching freshly returned report can journal this observation; ordinary artifact intake, agent text and historical runs cannot. Publication is coupled to that report's durable fingerprint. Exit code, start and timeout state and the initial detached checkout (when known) describe what the host observed, not coverage, checkout integrity after execution, acceptance-criteria satisfaction or authority. Research capture recovery, independent source corroboration, acceptance-criteria verification and deliberation integration remain open.

1. Frame a question, alternatives, acceptance criteria, scope, budget and relevant evidence.
2. Obtain independent initial assessments before presenting a facilitator's favored answer or another participant's proposal.
3. Share assessments and references under permission checks; distinguish observed facts, source claims, hypotheses, recommendations and unresolved questions.
4. Permit challenges, requests for missing evidence and revised proposals. Tests and research use ordinary governed execution, not invented findings.
5. Let participants persuade one another without a prescribed method or merit test. Expose available verification, expertise and genealogy as contextual information, not a weighting, disqualification or decision rule. Correlated opinions remain distinct from independently observed reality.
6. Preserve actual positions, reported changes of mind, disagreement, uncertainty and missing observations. Agreement is an internal outcome, not proof of truth or authorization.
7. Convey the participants' actual present understanding, including unresolved differences or a resource-limited outcome. Preserve dissent without a facilitator's justification test.
8. Only the execution policy can admit the resulting action. Existing unanimous two-party agreement gates, approval requirements and investigation limits remain intact.

Reuse the dispute runtime, [genealogy governance](GENEALOGY_GOVERNANCE.md), plan review and artifact provenance where applicable without importing their effect gates or review rules as epistemic judges. Missing participants or unpriced calls do not silently become agreement. No quorum or ballot determines the collective's understanding. Existing action permissions and safety constraints remain engine-owned regardless of persuasion. Reality can correct the collective through new observations and reconsideration, not by the host rewriting its beliefs.

The Queen may frame, synthesize, request clarification or facilitate revision. Leadership can shift to the specialist best qualified for the current problem; facilitation and verification should be separate when possible. The Queen may still coordinate execution through established policy paths. She cannot manufacture facts, override dissent, alter locked permissions or declare an external effect approved.

## 7. From thought to action

Advisory reasoning can finish with a direct answer and no workflow. When actions or verification are necessary, the collective proposes a plan using existing orchestration, validated DAGs, resource selection, provider routing and policy. Specialists can recommend changes or request assistance while execution is active. Amendments retain completed work and provenance, preserve existing approval boundaries, and undergo the existing graph/requirements validation. Do not silently replace a plan the Keeper approved or repeat an uncertain external dispatch.

A workflow may express dependent tasks, independent evidence collection, testing, nested workflows and explicit human decisions. Its lifecycle is independent of the collective's existence. A failed or completed run does not erase the specialists or their conversations. Outcomes inform subsequent reasoning through permitted memory and evidence pathways.

## 8. One external intelligence

Ask The Aive in Keeper Projects ranks enabled agent roles from the current catalog using deterministic description relevance and initially consults up to two, with further eligible peers available for requested conversation. It constructs text-only voices from configured model adapters, honors explicit Source pins, and prefers a different Source and then a different known configured model identifier for the second specialist when possible. This is an identifier-level diversity heuristic, not a model-family or evidence-independence check. It obtains independent-in-prompt opening positions, enables reciprocal conversation and requested further responses, then obtains a facilitator's report of their present understanding. A single configured Source/model is recorded alongside each successful assessment; different Sources do not establish independent model families or evidence. The Keeper sees one answer, missing participants and every actual redacted specialist assessment without opening the provenance inspector. The facilitator does not adjudicate disagreement; the host does not count votes, score validity or suppress contributions because they differ from the synthesis. Specialists may express any position or method of persuasion. Separately budgeted excerpts from the latest recorded positions of up to four participants, including reported persuasion and its source when available, are included in the same conversation's follow-up prompts, even after a failed turn; excerpts are labeled unverified and potentially from an earlier turn. This is bounded carry-forward, not a durable unresolved-objection registry or a proof of consensus. No answer is invented if no specialist contributed or synthesis is refused. Each attempted call uses existing price, spend, Source-room and pacing gates, and rechecks role configuration before admission; the fixed planning call-count gate is not a deliberation gate. Each participant receives its own passive memory tags and durable bounded attention from the entire collective memory. Conversation identities are provenance and attention context, never permissions. Old conversation grant fields are ignored and removed on write. The local transactive directory resolves query-relevant consolidated tag provenance against all retained collective conversation episodes, returning role and historical instance pointers with distinct episode counts (at most sixteen). Forgotten episodes and purged raw attribution cannot contribute to new lookups. Keeper questions, unknown identities and fabricated model text do not supply instance attribution. The host filters pointers against the current roster, reserves at most one of the two initial advisory slots for a relevant exact still-configured specialist instance, and leaves the other description-ranked for independent exploration; pins, diversity and call admission remain mandatory. All participating models receive at most six sanitized abbreviated pointers, not memory passages, verified expertise or new consultation authority. Historical identity may not be available; no additional inference or automatic contact occurs. Managed RECRUIT now also consults retained matching tag sections in the exact originating project/run's physical bank. Actual managed provider messages are labeled with their host-bound specialist identity at capture (including reconnect when the existing binding is available); Keeper messages, prompts, thoughts, mixed-source sections, purged data and unknown identities do not qualify. No additional model call, cross-run discovery, or dormant activation is granted. This is observed topic exposure, not verified skill; pre-attribution managed history remains unknown. The separate directional collaboration evidence projection derives counts from the existing retained mailbox receipts (24-hour retention, 128-entry global cap). Only exact same-project/run/provider ordinary root inquiry/request receipts with host-attributed specialist instances and sessions qualify. It reports claim/transport outcomes, distinct accepted requests with an authored direct advisory reply, and distinct requests with a transport-accepted return to the exact original session and instance. Objections count equally with supporting advice, duplicate replies cannot increase the request count, and silence/ambiguous transport are not competence failures. Groups, wakes, cross-run mail and unknown attribution are excluded. Keeper inspection in consultation settings exposes counts only, not payloads. RECRUIT may use positive returned-request counts only to break an exact description-relevance and pending-pressure tie among otherwise eligible current instances, with at least two observed returns for every tied candidate; unknown or sparse evidence leaves deterministic baseline unchanged. No task-quality, reading, agreement, causal contribution or general collaboration success rate is inferred. Evidence expires with its source receipts and project replacement; no additional reputation database or model call. Actual returned specialist and facilitator replies are redacted and banked separately as unverified agent notes, with role, instance and configured model route attribution, and the actual Keeper question is banked once as a user prompt. The existing queue and consolidation engine perform indexing and contradiction preservation. Capture retains up to 12,000 characters per reply before the 1,200-character transcript display cap; no generated system prompt, injected memory cue or recalled passage is copied into the deposit. Each returned reply and the first actual question are staged in the existing bounded crash journal before the next model call. Deposits become recallable only after the turn exits (including completed contributions before cancellation); startup recovers interrupted turns using exact utterance IDs, including a crash after queue commit but before journal removal. Recovery skips live captures. Clearing memory removes staged contributions, so later turn release cannot resurrect them. Disabled memory refuses new capture and release; previously captured entries remain recoverable when enabled. The journal's existing capacity limit and the interval before the provider reply reaches the capture hook remain limits; capture refuses an unconfirmed journal write rather than silently discarding it. The isolated conversation bank has no project membership or role-bank write and is governed by the existing memory retention/forget controls; deleting a chat transcript does not delete its separately banked experiences. Historical transcript turns are never automatically replayed. The Keeper can explicitly import retained questions, specialist excerpts and facilitator excerpts through the local chat panel, only into a conversation bank that has never been registered and has no staged capture. The import queues one atomic batch, leaves historical event times and absent model/instance metadata unknown, and does not consult other conversations. Previously banked, cleared and mixed-era conversations are refused because the old ledger cannot distinguish forgotten utterances from never-banked ones. A crash after registry creation but before batch commit conservatively refuses retry; no forgotten content is recreated. The runtime saves completed redacted turns under host-created conversation identities in local chunked settings (up to twelve conversations and sixteen turns each). Follow-ups use stored history, not caller-supplied history; the panel can reopen, start and delete conversations. Cancellation does not append a completed turn. Workflow replacement and clear-data operations clear the ledger. This is on-demand advisory activation, not a new managed task, tool grant or background inference loop. Each local advisory voice has a deterministic instance identity derived from the serialized role revision and ordered configured model route. The identity survives conversation and process boundaries, changes with configuration, and scopes the participant's attention separately from other instances of the same role. Role bank identity records provenance only. Successful assessments record instance provenance; the local bounded profile ledger retains up to 128 recently observed instances with answered, empty, failed and timed-out invocation counts. Admission denials and cancellation are not scored as model failures; counts describe observed response availability, not truth, agreement, task quality or collaborative reliability. The inspector displays the counts, and clear-data/import replacement erases the ledger. Managed-session instance identity, relationships, skills, model-family/genealogy verification, autonomous managed-task recruitment, unified messaging and authenticated API streaming remain open.

The primary interaction is a durable conversation with The Aive. The Keeper's prompt becomes an objective/question, not necessarily a workflow launch. Adopt useful Hermes-Agent patterns as capabilities, not an imported second orchestration system. Evaluate upstream implementation/licensing and adapt through existing interfaces.

| Isolated execution environments and worktrees | Existing workspace/executor isolation and approval policies |

A procedure is remembered experience of how to do something. Repeated useful experiences become habits; low-cost, reliable, approved habits may replay through existing mechanisms. Keep the original distinction: formation through repetition/consolidation is not a permission promotion. Strength affects recall; policy determines what executes. Preserve version, provenance, inputs, expected outputs, limits, observed outcomes, failure modes and correction history. Only a Keeper-approved current version can execute through the existing habit replay policy; mutations remain ordinary gated steps. No autonomous privilege increase, self-modification, silent code installation or model training merely because a skill performed well.

A reflex is the cheapest bounded safe response to a recognized condition, not permission to act outside policy. Unexpected input, changed permissions, version drift, failure, disagreement or uncertainty return the task to deliberate reasoning. Tests must be independently derived, and learned behavior must be reversible. Reuse the existing [procedural memory](PROCEDURAL_MEMORY.md), [habit formation](HABITS_AS_MEMORY.md), and [self-improvement](SELF_IMPROVEMENT.md) contracts, including their superseding memory-model notes.

## 10. Idle cognition and consolidation

The collective may carry authorized ongoing responsibilities: review unresolved questions, surface relevant cues, consolidate experience, maintain skills and check scheduled conditions. This is a finite, event-driven maintenance loop, not permanent billable thought. A paused/offline system has no fictitious cognition. On unsupported platforms, state the limitation and resume only permitted work on return.

Use existing banking/attention rules. Memory clerks retain and organize observed experience, not newly invented beliefs; contradictions remain available. Active reasoning may resolve a contradiction and its resolution is banked with provenance like other experience. Preserve deletion/retention choices and isolation. No automatic external transmission during idle consolidation.

## 11. Swarm UI and observable reality

The world depicts the living collective, not an animated workflow masquerading as intelligence. Preserve [the ecology's binding rule](../Ecology-and-the-Swarm.md). Cognitive links and workflow dependency edges must be visually and semantically distinct. A cognitive link is caused by actual communication/evidence events; a workflow dependency still describes execution order. Show queued vs delivered vs answered, uncertainty vs agreement, and waiting vs active inference. Provider thinking is not the same as deliberate peer speech.
